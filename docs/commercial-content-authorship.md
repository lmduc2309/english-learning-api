# Commercial content authorship register

This register is intentionally empty. A source must not be changed to
`commercial_status: approved` in `data/commercial-source-registry.json` until
the company holds a signed agreement covering the contributed definitions,
translations, examples, pronunciations, and revisions.

For every contributor, retain outside the repository:

- legal name and contact details;
- agreement identifier and signature date;
- work-for-hire or copyright-assignment language valid in the contributor's
  jurisdiction;
- an express commercial, worldwide, transferable and sublicensable grant;
- confirmation that the contribution is original and does not copy a
  restricted dictionary or model output;
- covered batch IDs and content hashes;
- reviewer identity and review date.

Do not commit contracts or personal information. Record only the agreement ID,
covered batch digest, approval date, and approving officer below.
