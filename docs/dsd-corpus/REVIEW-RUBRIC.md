# DSD Review Rubric

Every publishable textual and IPA record needs a reviewer who is **not** the
author (invariant 6). This is what the reviewer checks.

## What review is for

Review is the evidence that DSD content was independently checked, and it is
what makes the corpus defensible as original work. A reviewer who approves in
bulk without reading produces provenance records that assert something untrue —
which is worse than no review, because it manufactures confidence.

If you cannot review at the rate being asked, say so. The gate exists to be
slow.

## Eligibility

- You are not the author of the record.
- You are not the same person under a second contributor ID.
- You hold executed IP-assignment evidence and an active registry entry.
- You have the language competence for what you are reviewing. Reviewing
  Vietnamese requires native or near-native Vietnamese.

## English definition

- [ ] Defines the sense rather than restating the headword.
- [ ] Comprehensible to a learner; no rarer word than the headword.
- [ ] One sense per record. Two senses in one definition is a rejection.
- [ ] Part of speech matches the definition.
- [ ] No Wiktionary-style pipe labels, template debris, or HTML entities.
- [ ] No copied or paraphrased phrasing from any source.

## Vietnamese translation

- [ ] Translates the *sense*, not the English word in isolation.
- [ ] Natural Vietnamese, not word-order-preserving calque.
- [ ] Correct register for a learner dictionary.
- [ ] Diacritics correct and complete.
- [ ] No CJK characters, including punctuation such as `。` and `，`.
- [ ] Not identical to the English.
- [ ] Not machine output pasted in.

## Bilingual example

- [ ] Demonstrates the sense in use rather than merely containing the headword.
- [ ] Natural English and natural Vietnamese; the pair matches in meaning.
- [ ] Self-contained — comprehensible without surrounding context.
- [ ] Free of names, brands, and anything that dates or localises it oddly.
- [ ] Appropriate for all audiences.

## IPA

- [ ] en-US, in the agreed transcription convention.
- [ ] Stress marked.
- [ ] Valid IPA characters only; no ASCII substitutes.
- [ ] Matches the headword as spelled.
- [ ] Machine candidates were consulted as hints only. The record is authored.

## Audio

- [ ] Correct headword, clearly articulated.
- [ ] No clipping, truncation, or artefacts.
- [ ] Consistent level with the rest of the batch.
- [ ] Generated by an approved voice — never Amy or Ryan.

Every released asset gets an individual human listening decision. Sampling is an
additional checkpoint, not a substitute (F4, accepted product cost).

## Decisions

| Decision | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `approved` | Meets every applicable criterion |
| `rejected` | Does not meet one or more; record which |
| `needs_changes` | Returned to the author with specific notes |

Record `reviewed_by` as your pseudonymous contributor ID, plus the timestamp and
the content hash you reviewed. If the content changes afterwards, the hash no
longer matches and the approval is automatically invalidated — approve the
version you actually read.

## What you may not do

- Approve your own work under any identity.
- Approve in bulk without reading each record.
- Consult a blocked source to check a definition. If you need to verify a fact,
  use an approved reference and disclose it.
- Rely on the similarity audit as a quality check. It measures overlap with the
  legacy corpus, not correctness.
